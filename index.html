01 — Profile

Science–policy relations, research evaluation and open science.

My work sits at the intersection of science policy, research evaluation and scholarly communication.

I investigate how scientific knowledge is produced, communicated, discovered and reused within public policy environments. I am particularly interested in policy citations and mentions as traces of science–policy interaction, and in the research infrastructures, metadata practices and information systems that shape research visibility.

I am a PhD candidate in Information Sciences at University North, Croatia, and a research librarian at the Institute for Development and International Relations (IRMO) in Zagreb.

02 — Doctoral research

Understanding how research travels into policy.

My doctoral research examines how scientific knowledge becomes visible in international and European policy environments.

The dissertation investigates policy citations and mentions as indicators of knowledge transfer between science and policy. Rather than treating them only as measures of societal impact, I examine the infrastructures, metadata ecosystems and information practices that determine which research becomes visible, traceable and reusable in policy documents.

The project combines scientometric analysis, policy-document analysis and research infrastructure studies, drawing on policy-mention and scholarly metadata sources.

04 — Professional activities

Research librarianship at IRMO.

Alongside my doctoral research, I work as a research librarian at the Institute for Development and International Relations in Zagreb.

My professional work connects library practice with research support, scholarly communication and open science. It includes research information management, researcher identity and metadata support, research visibility, bibliometric guidance and support for institutional research workflows.
