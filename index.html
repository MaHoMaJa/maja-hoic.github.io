01 — About

Science–policy relations and research impact

I am a PhD candidate in Information Sciences and a librarian at the Institute for Development and International Relations in Zagreb. My research examines the relationship between science and public policy, with particular attention to how scholarly research is cited, mentioned and used in international and European policy environments.

I work with policy-mention and altmetric data to investigate knowledge flows, science diplomacy, open science and the broader societal impact of scientific publications.

02 — Policy citations

Policy citations as traces of research use

Policy mentions offer a way to observe how scientific knowledge enters institutional and public documents.

My work treats these mentions not only as impact indicators, but as evidence of the infrastructures, actors and processes through which knowledge becomes visible in policy settings.
